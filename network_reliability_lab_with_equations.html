🔄 MTBF & MTTR

MTBF measures hardware/system reliability over time. Higher is better.
MTTR measures team response and repair speed. Lower is better.

⏱️ RTO & RPO

RTO (Time): How quickly systems must recover after disaster.
RPO (Data): Maximum tolerable age of unbacked-up data loss.

📐 Reliability Equations & Metric Formula Reference

MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures)

MTBF = (Total Operational Time - Total Downtime) ÷ Number of Failures

Calculates average duration a component or system runs reliably without failing.

MTTR (Mean Time to Repair)

MTTR = Total Downtime ÷ Number of Failures

Measures operational efficiency and the speed at which engineers diagnose and restore service.

System Availability Uptime (%)

Availability % = [(Total Operational Time - Total Downtime) ÷ Total Operational Time] × 100

Determines overall percentage of time network resources remain accessible to end users.

RTO (Recovery Time Objective)

RTO Compliance = Actual Incident Downtime ≤ Targeted SLA Limit

Target duration set by Business SLAs defining the maximum allowable time systems can remain down.

RPO (Recovery Point Objective)

RPO Compliance = Actual Data Loss Window ≤ Targeted SLA Limit

Maximum acceptable amount of data lost, determined by backup/replication frequency.

Total Revenue Financial Loss

Financial Loss = Total Outage Downtime (Hours) × Business Cost Rate ($ / Hour)

Quantifies financial impact imposed on operations and revenue streams during periods of downtime.
